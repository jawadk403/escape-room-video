## The cast

Ten reference portraits lock every face across every clip: Anna at fifty (tired,
then rested, then in her green dress at the reception), Anna and David twenty
years younger for the opening, plus Rose, Claire, David, his wife and the
wedding guest. Nobody changes face, hair or wardrobe inside a scene.

## How it was made

- **Dialogue** — Seedance 2.5 at 480p, omni_reference, speaking the lines
  itself, so the lip-sync is its own and each character keeps one voice.
- **Narration** — the script's three narrator blocks were recorded as one
  continuous take each, never stitched per sentence, and laid under silent
  b-roll in which nobody's mouth moves.
- **Closing product section (last 44 s)** — composited in code from the real
  product photograph rather than generated, so the tube's flat crimped seal is
  correct. Every text reveal is pinned to a word time measured off the
  finished voiceover.

## Quality checks

Every clip was transcribed segment by segment and diffed against the script;
all 30 passed with no missing lines. Three were regenerated after the check
caught real faults — a mispronounced brand name, a substituted word in Anna's
closing line, and a wrong reply in the reveal scene. Four unscripted
vocalisations the model added between lines are silenced in place, so removing
them introduced no cut.

Captions are read back off the final audio, never grouped across a pause, held
to one line inside the safe zone, and cleared off all four time cards and the
product section, which carry their own typography.
