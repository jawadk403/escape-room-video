# YURI — "She's gotta be on something" / coffee shop

1080x1920, 30 fps, 6:48, Seedance 2.5 voices (original, unchanged).
Two versions: **clean** (no captions) and **captioned** (burned-in).

Copied beat for beat from the 2:53 coffee-shop reference, with the cast swapped:
the bodybuilder becomes a striking 60-year-old woman and the two young guys
become two white American women. Fillers replace steroids, skincare replaces
diet, and the Yuri tube replaces the mineral bottle.

## How to download

Each master is ~210 MB, over GitHub's 100 MB per-file limit, so each is split
into five 7z volumes. **Download all five of the version you want**, keep them
in one folder, then extract **`.001` only** — it rebuilds the single MP4 by
itself. Stored with no compression (`-mx=0`), so extraction is instant and the
MP4 comes out bit-identical to the master.
