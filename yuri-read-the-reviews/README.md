## Build

- **Model:** Seedance 2.5, omni-reference, 480p 9:16, `generate_audio: true`.
  Every voice is Seedance's own — no voice change, no dubbing.
- **Presenter:** Option 3, consistent across all 15 talking clips (same face,
  hair, pink lipstick, blush, black tee, necklaces, vanity and ring light).
- **Product:** the Yuri tube is rendered from one locked reference so the
  shape, pale pink, gold band, clear base cap and white `YURI` lettering are
  identical in every shot.
- **Structure:** 26 beats, matching the reference — the presenter's audio runs
  continuously and 15 b-roll cutaways sit over it, plus the review screenshot
  and the end card.
- **Captions:** white rounded box, black bold, one line, box top at 73.4% of
  frame height — measured off the reference frame, not estimated.
- **Script:** every dialogue clip was transcribed with Whisper and diffed
  against the supplied script. All lines are word-perfect, including
  "seven years" (not seventeen).

## Brand rules observed

Clear transparent gel applied with the brush, dried into an unbroken
transparent film peeled off by hand — no fabric sheet mask, no eye or mouth
holes anywhere. Competitor products appear plain and unbranded.
